Keyring privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Who uses Keyring

Keyring is a personal app used only by its owner. It is not offered to other people.

Google data

  • With the owner's permission, Keyring reads and organises email in the owner's own Gmail accounts (read, archive and delete) so the owner can see them in one inbox and quickly find sign-in codes.
  • Access tokens and cached messages are encrypted on the owner's device with a key derived from the owner's master password before they are stored. The server only holds encrypted data.
  • Google data is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • Keyring's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Keyring's Inbox Settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting an account deletes its stored tokens and cached messages.
